Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for Kenyan businesses.

Is PaperPilot free?

You can try it without signing up. Free PDFs include a small PaperPilot footer. Paid branding and unlimited features are planned, but payment-based feature unlocking is not fully implemented yet.

How does M-Pesa payment work?

Choose the paid option, enter your email and M-Pesa number, then approve the STK Push prompt on your phone. Keep the order reference. Do not treat the prompt alone as proof of a completed payment.

Is my data stored?

Document details are currently processed in your browser; saved business and client shortcuts are stored locally on this device. Do not use a shared device for confidential client data. Cloud account-based document storage is not yet implemented.

Are PaperPilot PDFs KRA/eTIMS-compliant?

No. PaperPilot currently generates ordinary PDFs and does not transmit invoices to KRA through eTIMS. If your business must issue eTIMS invoices, use a KRA eTIMS solution or a properly integrated approved system.
